Namaz Board privacy

Namaz Board is a small, approval-only app for friends to log their daily namaz. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else.

What's stored

  • Your Google email and a user ID, to sign you in and check you were approved.
  • The name you choose, your city or rounded location, and your prayer-time settings.
  • Which namaz you log each day, and your streak numbers.
  • Days you mark as excused.
  • If you turn on alerts, a push subscription so your phone can receive them.

Who sees what

  • Public (🌍): everyone on Namaz Board sees your name, streak, grade and points on the Everyone board. Daily details are not shown there.
  • Private (🔒): you're hidden from the Everyone board.
  • Groups: people in your groups see your name, streak, grade and points. In full details groups they also see which namaz you logged each day.
  • Excused days are visible only to you. Nobody else, including the app's owner, can see them. Others only see your final streak numbers.
  • Location: if you tap "Use my location", only a position rounded to about 10 km is saved, together with a label such as "Near London". It's never shown on anyone's screen.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Push notifications go through your phone's own notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla). The owner keeps an encrypted weekly backup, which never includes excused days.

Deleting your data

In the app, open ⚙️ Settings and tap Delete my account. Your profile, logs, streaks, excused days and alerts are deleted straight away. Copies in old backups expire within 90 days.
