Namaz Board privacy

Namaz Board is a private, invite-only app for a small group of friends to log their daily namaz.

What we store

  • Your Google account email and a user ID, used only to sign you in and check you were invited.
  • The display name, city and prayer-time settings you choose.
  • Which namaz you log each day.
  • If you turn on alerts, a push subscription so your phone can receive notifications.

Who can see it

Other invited members of the board can see your name, streaks and logs. If you turn off "show my extras", your sunnah, nafl and tahajjud are hidden from them. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else.

Where it lives

Data is stored in Supabase (database and sign-in). The app is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

Ask the board owner to remove you, and your account and logs are deleted.
